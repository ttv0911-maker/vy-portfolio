As my company partnered with the brand to launch its franchise in Vietnam, I designed a local retail website that brings its identity, products and lifestyle stories together. The goal was to help visitors discover furniture for their homes and move naturally from online browsing to an enquiry or showroom visit.

Project Context

Sierra Roots Stone is the manufacturing company behind Artifex Stone. The factory needed its own website to reach potential customers, introduce its production capabilities and present its products and materials. My role was to turn that starting point into a clear digital experience, from UI/UX design to an AI-assisted front-end build.

The Challenge

01+

Researching a new industry

I had to research how factory websites communicate: what potential customers look for, how to present manufacturing capabilities and how to connect technical information with a clear enquiry path.

02○

Starting without a system

There was no existing brand guideline or design system to work from. Visual direction, typography, colour and reusable components had to be established alongside the website.

03✳

Keeping every page consistent

The homepage, capabilities and material pages serve different purposes. The challenge was to give each one the right content hierarchy while keeping the overall experience coherent.

Design System

A shared visual foundation for a clear, consistent manufacturing website.
