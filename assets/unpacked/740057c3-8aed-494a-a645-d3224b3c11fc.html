KidLoop connects parents so children's clothes get a second life instead of a landfill — safe, affordable, and good for the planet.
OUR STORY
It started with a wardrobe. Two mums, three kids, and a mountain of barely-worn clothes that were outgrown in a season. Passing them on took weeks of messages, meetups, and guesswork — so we built the thing we wished existed.
Today KidLoop is a marketplace where every listing is quality-checked, every order ships free, and every outfit gets to keep going.
