Introduction

Artifex Stone stepped into its first physical exhibitions in 2026 and needed on-brand printed collateral — banners, standees, and product catalogues — to introduce its stone collections to architects, designers, and trade buyers across three exhibitions, including VIFA Expo 2026.

Results

The VIFA Expo 2026 booth generated a lead that converted into a 220 million VND order — the collateral's first direct, measurable business outcome.
