Key Takeaways

01 — Start with structure

As my first UI/UX project, I learned that a clear information architecture should come before visual design.

02 — Design for real content

Working with bilingual copy, product data and imagery showed me how strongly content affects layout decisions.

03 — Make handoff explicit

I learned to communicate interactions, responsive behavior and content requirements more clearly to developers.

04 — Document the process

Because I did not retain the original sitemap and wireframes, I learned to preserve design decisions, iterations and handoff files throughout future projects.
